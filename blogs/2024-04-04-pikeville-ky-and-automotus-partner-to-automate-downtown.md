Pikeville, KY – The City of Pikeville and Automotus, Inc. (Automotus), the leader in automated curb management solutions, are embarking on a transformative parking enforcement journey set to reshape the city’s downtown landscape. Through this initiative, Automotus has integrated 25 cameras to oversee parking activities across 75 time-limited on-street parking spaces in the downtown area.
